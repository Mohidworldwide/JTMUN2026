02 / DELEGATES

Delegation

A delegation must contain between 6 and 10 delegates. The first delegate registered will be the Head Delegate. Each delegate must be assigned a committee.

United Nations Security Council (UNSC) is a compulsory dual-delegate committee and must have exactly 2 delegates.

Global Crisis Cabinet is compulsory and must have exactly 1 delegate.
6 Delegates
Committee Requirements

Every delegation must include:

• 2 delegates in United Nations Security Council (UNSC)
• 1 delegate in Global Crisis Cabinet

All other committees may only have one delegate per team.
